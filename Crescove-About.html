ABOUT
ME

Hello, I'm Renz!

Welcome to Crescove, where you find you cove and embrace cresco!

I’m a 2nd year student taking Bachelor of Science in Information Technology with Area of Specialization in Web Development in Holy Angel University.

As a person who lived through experiences relating to mental health, such as depression, anxiety, stress, and burnout, I wanted to create a private medium where people can freely check and express themselves so that no one has to feel stuck and alone again when facing their problems.

To develop a digital embrace to cresco, to grow

“It is when we are at our lowest when we discover a deeper understanding of what would lift us up again.”

A website open for all ages, Crescove aims to provide users with a private solitude where engagement in the website’s activities allows them to assess their own wellbeing while also receiving feedback. It also allows them to easily access and explore related topics regarding their experiences or problems so that they gain new insights into their situation. As much as possible, I want the website to advocate for mental and emotional health awareness so that the users could find a space where they are understood.

The website's goal is to help the users achieve a better mental health state by providing them the necessary information to build their confidence. The provided activities would engage them to learn more about their true feelings, encouraging them to become more open about their mental health. Upon building their courage through the website's guidance, I hope they will find true advice and companionship in the real world or, at least, create a brand-new light in their journey to guide them out of their difficulties.
